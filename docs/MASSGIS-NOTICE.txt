Massachusetts municipal geography

Source credit: MassGIS (Bureau of Geographic Information), Commonwealth of Massachusetts EOTSS.

The municipality geometry in src/lib/massachusetts-map.generated.ts and scripts/massachusetts-municipal-geofences.generated.json, and the eight derived maps in public/images/maps/, use the MassGIS Municipalities general-coast data layer (TOWNSSURVEY_POLYM_GENCOAST). Project scripts transform the geometry into SVG paths, service-area geometry, and WebP region maps. These assets contain municipal geography, not MLS listings or MLS property photographs.

Official dataset description:
https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massgis-data-municipalities

MassGIS data-use policy permits redistribution and derivative works:
https://www.mass.gov/info-details/learn-about-massgis-data
https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massgis-frequently-asked-questions

The checked-in generators used this public ArcGIS-hosted mirror:
https://services8.arcgis.com/o4UVke7qqC8oNJ7j/ArcGIS/rest/services/Massachusetts_Towns_General_Coast/FeatureServer/0/query

The mirror identifies its source as MassGIS, reports a May 2024 source vintage and December 2024 access, and is published under ArcGIS item 7be85ad709d1484baff449096a3f0af3. The mirror itself is not an official MassGIS-owned ArcGIS account. This notice records the existing source chain; it does not imply that a fresh October 2026 official dataset was downloaded or that the geometry was independently compared point-for-point with the current official release.

Use the maps for application display and demonstration. For authoritative municipal boundary determination, consult the official source description and appropriate survey records. Do not interpret these display maps as a survey or warranty of boundary accuracy.
